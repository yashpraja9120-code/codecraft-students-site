about the project

Why CodeCraft Students exists

Most explanations of programming and AI are written by people who forgot what it felt like to not understand them yet. We're trying to fix that, one article at a time.

What this site is

CodeCraft Students is a small, independently run publication focused on one audience: students who are learning to code or learning how AI works, usually for the first time. Every article is written to be understood on its own, without requiring a textbook or a prerequisite course sitting next to it.

What we cover

The site is organized around four topics students consistently ask about: core programming fundamentals, artificial intelligence concepts explained without hype, practical developer tools like Git and code editors, and computer science basics like data structures. We'd rather publish ten genuinely useful guides than a hundred thin ones.

How articles get written

Each guide is drafted, then edited for clarity, then checked for accuracy against official documentation and reputable sources. Code examples are tested before publishing. We correct mistakes when readers point them out — if you spot one, the contact page is the fastest way to reach us.

How the site supports itself

CodeCraft Students is supported by display advertising, which keeps every article free to read with no account, subscription, or paywall. Ads are kept out of the way of the actual content, and we don't accept sponsored posts that would compromise the accuracy of a guide.

Who's behind it

The site is maintained by a small team of self-taught and formally trained developers who remember exactly which concepts were confusing the first time around, and try to explain them the way they wish someone had explained them.
